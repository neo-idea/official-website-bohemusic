隐私政策（中文）

最近更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日

1. 我们不收集你的个人数据

薄荷音乐没有账号系统，不上传你的任何个人数据到我们的服务器。歌曲搜索、榜单与流播直接与 Apple Music（MusicKit）通信，受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

2. 本地存储的数据

  • 歌单、收藏、播放历史：仅保存在你的设备本地，不上传到我们的服务器。
  • 设置与主题偏好：仅保存在本机（UserDefaults）。
  • 购买记录：由 Apple App Store 管理；App 仅在本机读取权益状态以解锁会员功能。
  • 诊断与埋点：崩溃日志（MetricKit）与匿名使用事件仅写入本地文件，可随时在「设置 → 导出数据」查看或删除，绝不上传。

3. 第三方服务

  • Apple Music / MusicKit：授权后读取你的音乐资料库以同步歌单与流播；可在系统设置随时撤销。
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：处理全部购买、订阅与退款。

我们不集成任何广告、统计或追踪 SDK，不使用追踪跨 App 的标识符（符合 App Tracking Transparency 透明度要求）。

4. 权限

  • 媒体与 Apple Music 资料（NSAppleMusicUsageDescription）：用于同步歌单与流播歌曲。拒绝后仍可播放随 App 提供的 Creative Commons 内置曲目。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集信息；因我们不收集任何数据，不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 你的权利

由于所有数据仅存于你的设备；Apple Music 资料库与订阅状态始终由 Apple 管理，你可以随时通过删除 App 来清除本机数据。如有疑问请联系 privacy@zpad.app。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: Oct 25, 2026

1. We do not collect your personal data

Bohe Music has no account system and never uploads your personal data to our servers. Catalog search, charts and streaming talk directly to Apple Music (MusicKit) under Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Data stored locally

  • Playlists, favorites, history: stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Settings & theme preference: on-device only (UserDefaults).
  • Purchases: managed by the App Store; the app only reads entitlement status locally to unlock member features.
  • Diagnostics & analytics: crash logs (MetricKit) and anonymous events are written to local files only — viewable and deletable via Settings → Export Data, never uploaded.

3. Third-party services

Apple Music / MusicKit and App Store / StoreKit. We embed no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and use no cross-app tracking identifiers.

4. Permissions

Media & Apple Music library access (NSMusicUsageDescription) is used to sync playlists and stream songs. If denied, the Creative Commons tracks bundled with the app remain fully playable.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Your rights

All app-created data lives on your device. Apple Music library and subscription data remain under Apple’s control; delete the app to remove local app data at any time. Contact privacy@zpad.app with questions.
